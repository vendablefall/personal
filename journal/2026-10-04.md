# 4 October 2026

**Jack, the Sunday that didn’t happen.** She asked him to reassure her he was still into her and wanted to hang Sunday. He did: “I do wanna hang tomorrow and I am into u.” He also said he wanted to take it a little slower — he’d overwhelmed himself last week, feelings hadn’t changed, energy low, lots of time. She agreed it had been going a bit fast. He wished her a good night if she went out. She told him the thing was around the corner, new crew, a little nervous going solo. Hearts from him.

Hours later, ~9pm at the party, she asked what time tomorrow — if he wanted to meet early she would have gone home early. No reply, so she stayed out all night. Sunday: “Hey, I guess today wasn’t the day, I hope you are okay.” Still nothing. As of Sunday 9pm, that’s the hang.

## What actually happened after Friday

Friday’s journal said give it till Saturday, then one light message if needed. They did reconnect. She sent that she wanted to be railed; he was into it. The anxious ask came next, earlier in the day. He answered it well. The time-ask was a separate, practical message from the party, not stacked on the anxiety. Then the Sunday closer. That was the last of it.

Tuesday postponed. Thursday no socialising after the gig. Friday he didn’t message when he said he would. Saturday he named Sunday. Sunday he didn’t pick a time, didn’t show, and didn’t answer the closer.

## Where her head’s at

This is a flake on a promised Sunday, not a mystery. Friday’s read was right about motive and wrong about follow-through. He did not use her and bounce. He ran hot, got overwhelmed, said so out loud, and then could not do the slower version either. The insecure voice was wrong that this is “he’s had his way.” It was right that something was already off. Both can be true. His body-wanting her was loud and real. His ability to keep a simple plan is not matching it.

The reassurance ask was one thing, earlier. He answered it. Hours later she asked for a time so she could leave the party if he wanted her early — ordinary planning, not a second anxiety dump. He didn’t answer that, so she stayed out. That ask did not make him disappear. What he failed was the Sunday he had already offered. Useful information, not a verdict on her.

Her closer was good. Warm, no chase. Plan: leave it there. If he comes back with another “I’ll buzz you / miss your smile,” the answer is a time or nothing — not another week of vibe.

The live question is no longer whether he likes her. It’s whether she wants a crush whose pattern is: intense, overwhelm, promise the next door, fail to walk through it. That pattern is now the main fact.

She is not standing on one leg. Allan is real. Her people are real. The room is hers. Jack was a lovely new thing that could not hold the week he started.
