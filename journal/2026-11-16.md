# 16 November 2026

**Phuket with the family, 24 Nov – 1 Dec.** Seven days in a villa with Dad (Murray), her brothers Gerard and Hugh, and Geoff, her uncle on Mum's side. Mum isn't on the list as she told it.

She's nervous, and the reasons are specific:

1. **First time spending real time with Dad since being Lexi.** Nearly five years out, six surgical rounds, a whole life rebuilt in Wellington, and this is the first stretch of days with him in the room. It's not a dinner she can leave after two hours; it's a week in a shared house.
2. **First overseas trip as Lexi that's actually a holiday.** Korea and Hollywood were surgery trips, with recovery rooms and follow-ups. This is the first time she's just a woman on holiday abroad, with family, with no medical reason to be there.

What she wants is clear: a chill, relaxed holiday. She's seen all the temples in Phuket before. She wants to rent a motorbike and plan her *own* holiday inside the group one, not be beholden to five men's schedule. The bike is partly transport and mostly autonomy: the ability to leave any dinner, any beach, any conversation, under her own power.

## Reading it

The "own holiday" plan is not avoidance; it's the right structure. A week of forced togetherness is how family trips go wrong for anyone, and she's carrying more into this one than most. Having a daily shape that is hers by default and joins the group by choice means every shared moment is chosen, not endured. That's better for her *and* for Dad.

Worth noticing: the promise at 11 was *run away from my family and start a new life as the real me*. She did the second half. This week is the first time the real her sits down with her father for seven days. That's not a small thing, and nerves are the correct response. It doesn't need to go perfectly. It needs to go, and she needs to come home still herself.
