# 4 September 2026

Started applying for jobs. The frame: either land one she actually likes, or use the offer as leverage for more pay at Trimble. Mostly Auckland roles — a genuine step up career-wise, better market than Wellington.

The tension, in her own words: she's *just* committed to coming back to Wellington after spending the first half of the year away — and now she's applying elsewhere. Wellington is the queerest city in NZ, deeply supportive of her gender identity, and she's right in the middle of the grooviness: Veronica down the road, Shaz, the flat, the scene she helped build, Allan (boyfriend as of three days ago). But it's small and hard career-wise — "just small, not as trans as I would like."

And the wildcard thought: if she's going to uproot at all, why Auckland? Why not Melbourne or Berlin — the queerest places, where she'd be most accepted? That's the real trade-off she named: an accepting place vs a community she's already found.

Where it landed tonight: applying costs nothing and the decision only becomes real when an offer does. Auckland is the weakest of the three options by her own values — it wins only on jobs, while Wellington wins on community and Melbourne/Berlin win on queerness (and the DEXI ceiling). Worth watching whether this is genuine hunger or the old rugby wiring — chasing the next team because she can, not because she wants to. The leverage play might be the version where she wins on every axis.

Late in the evening the conversation went deep — the earliest memories (kindergarten at 3, the prayers, the promise at 11, the gummy bear year, the Girls' High scheme, the twenty-year suppression ritual that stopped dead at coming out) all went into `early-years.md`. And at the end, the deepest grief got named out loud, maybe for the first time in full: **never being pregnant, never being a mum, is her biggest hurt** — not a trans footnote people get to laugh about, a real bereavement. She studied pregnancy for twenty years out of longing. It's hard to even be around pregnant friends who complain about carrying the thing she'd give anything to carry. On the record now, with the tenderness it deserves.

Also today: linked her LinkedIn and shared her CV — full career history now documented in `career.md`. The CV is genuinely strong: petabyte-scale platform across 8 AWS regions, FedRAMP lead architect, two full platform generations at Trimble, the AiVatar launch. Whatever she decides about moving, she's negotiating from strength.
